Assumptions and open questions

No car parking

The ground floor uses the full 20′ width. The 3′ front setback only fits two-wheelers. If you need a car, the usual fix is stilt parking with the house on floors 1 and 2.

Check local bylaws

This plan assumes a 3′ front setback, 2′ rear setback and no side setbacks (shared walls). That gives about 83% ground coverage. Many authorities cap coverage at 60–75% and require a side setback on plots this narrow. Confirm with your BBMP, GHMC, BDA or local authority before drawing working plans.

Light and air

Both side walls are party walls, so windows are only on the south and north faces. Baths 1 and 2 sit on the west party wall and need exhaust fans ducted to the terrace. The powder room has a north ventilator.

North glass wall

North light is soft and even, with no direct sun, so the double-height glass stays cool. The rear compound wall is only 2′ away, so it gets a planted green wall to look at. Use toughened laminated glass and add privacy film on the lower panes if a neighbour overlooks it.

Double-height living

The ceiling is about 19½′ high. Use a ceiling fan on a long downrod or a large-blade fan, and a pendant light on a 7′ drop. The gallery rail should be 3′6″ high in toughened laminated glass.

Study

The first-floor study (10′ × 12′) has a north window and is reached by a short walkway from the gallery. It can become Bedroom 3 later.
